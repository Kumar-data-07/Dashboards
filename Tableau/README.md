# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project created using **Tableau** to analyze employee demographics, attrition, job satisfaction, and workforce distribution.

The dashboard provides HR teams and business stakeholders with a clear view of employee attrition patterns and workforce characteristics, helping identify areas that may require further investigation.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Understand attrition patterns across departments
- Compare attrition between male and female employees
- Analyze employee distribution across different age groups
- Examine employee education fields
- Understand job satisfaction across different job roles
- Identify age groups with higher attrition
- Provide an interactive HR analytics dashboard for decision-making

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Excel/CSV** – Data source and data preparation
- **Calculated Fields** – KPI and metric calculations
- **Tableau Filters & Slicers** – Interactive analysis
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPIs, and comparative analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Department-wise Attrition

A pie chart showing employee attrition across:

- Human Resources
- Research & Development
- Sales

This helps identify which departments contribute most to overall attrition.

---

### 2. Employee Distribution by Age Group

A bar chart showing the number of employees across different age groups.

The analysis helps understand the organization's workforce composition and identify the most represented age ranges.

---

### 3. Job Satisfaction Rating

A detailed table comparing **Job Roles** against **Job Satisfaction Ratings**.

The analysis includes satisfaction ratings from **1 to 4** and provides a Grand Total for each job role.

This helps identify differences in employee satisfaction across roles.

---

### 4. Attrition by Gender

The dashboard compares attrition between:

- Female employees
- Male employees

This provides a quick view of gender-based differences in employee attrition.

---

### 5. Education Field-wise Attrition

A horizontal bar chart analyzing attrition across different education fields, including:

- Life Sciences
- Medical
- Marketing
- Technical Degree
- Other
- Human Resources

This helps identify education fields associated with higher employee attrition.

---

### 6. Attrition Rate by Gender and Age Group

The dashboard uses donut charts to analyze attrition across different age groups:

- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Over 55

Each age group is further divided by gender to provide a more detailed understanding of attrition patterns.
